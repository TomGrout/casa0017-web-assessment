          How sign-up works

          You'll get a WhatsApp message; reply YES to confirm. Updates come when the status rises (straight away) or eases (after an hour), with a link to the 7-day forecast. Reply STATUS any time, or send a location pin to move your area.

          About Live ThameSafe Spill Map

          Proactive, speedy and informative sewage risk assessments for Thames River users.

          The traffic light shows how likely sewage from storm overflows is on your stretch of the Thames, now and for the next 7 days. It is a model estimate with stated uncertainty, not a water test. EDM monitors indicate, not confirm, spills. Check official bathing-water advice before entering the water.
